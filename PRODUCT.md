# KnicksIQ

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Knicks fans revisiting the 2025–26 season. Inferred from the existing README and public archive copy; no new audience was specified.

## Product Purpose

Ask season questions in plain language, review the evidence behind an answer, and explore games and reviewed reports.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing routes: archive, games, reports, analyst. The archive covers the 2025–26 regular season and postseason to the extent supported by available data. Preserve readiness checks, conversation persistence, answer citations, errors and retries. Do not fabricate scores, records, testimonials, or data coverage. Public UI avoids infrastructure terminology.

## Brand Commitments

Existing KnicksIQ name, mark, Knicks blue and orange. An unofficial fan project with no NBA or Knicks affiliation. Retaining these assets is a reconstruction assumption drawn from the repository.

## Evidence on Hand

apps/web/README.md; apps/web/src/features/knicks/archive.tsx; apps/web/src/styles/theme.css; existing Madison Square Garden photography credited to With Paul on Unsplash.

## Product Principles

- Let fans ask in their own words.
- Keep answers and supporting evidence together.
- Make data limitations visible.
- Keep navigation and question entry available on narrow screens.
